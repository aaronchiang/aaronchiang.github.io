這個 Google OAuth 應用程式只供網站作者本人使用：讓自家的 Home Assistant 讀取本人的 Google 日曆，顯示在家中的牆面面板。資料只存在家中的 Home Assistant，不會分享給任何第三方，也不會用於其他用途。可以隨時在 Google 帳號的「第三方存取權」移除授權。

This Google OAuth application is used only by the author of this site, to let his own Home Assistant read his own Google Calendar and show it on a wall panel at home. The data stays on that Home Assistant at home. It is not shared with any third party and is not used for any other purpose. Access can be removed at any time under "Third-party access" in the Google Account.
